ABOUT

NATHAN
FISCHER

I'm Nathan, a sports and wildlife photographer based around Greenville, South Carolina.

On game nights I'm on the sideline shooting football and cross country — full-game coverage, edited and delivered fast. Outside of that, I spend time photographing wildlife around the area for my own portfolio.

Every shoot is shot on a Nikon D7100 with a 70–300mm telephoto, and edited in Adobe Lightroom before it goes out.
