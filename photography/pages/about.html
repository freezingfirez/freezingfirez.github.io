I'm a student photographer based in Travelers Rest, South Carolina, and photography has been my way of slowing down a world that moves too fast. I started with a borrowed point-and-shoot at car shows and haven't stopped since.

My main interests pull me in three directions: the geometry and aggression of cars at meets and on the road, the silence of a dark field waiting for the Milky Way core to clear the treeline, and the craft of making a house look like someone's dream home. Three subjects — one consistent eye.

Everything is shot on a Nikon D7100. I believe in working a camera until you know exactly what it'll give you in any light. No excuses from gear — just decisions from the shooter.

This portfolio is part of a longer project: growing into a photography business while still in school. If you have a car you want shot, a property that needs listing photos, or want prints from any of these galleries, reach out.
