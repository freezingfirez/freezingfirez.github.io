I'm Nathan, a student in Travelers Rest, SC who got into photography with a 2009 DSLR and interest in astronomy. Everything I do is for fun, not as a business.

Most of what you'll see here is cars (meets, rolling shots, details) and astronomy when I can get away from light pollution.

Everything's on a Nikon D7100, or a ZWO Seestar S50.

If I photographed your car at a meet, check Your Photos — I usually drop galleries there so you can download the shots. Otherwise, poke around the galleries and grab whatever you like.
