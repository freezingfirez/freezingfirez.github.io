I'm Nathan — a student in Travelers Rest, SC who got into photography at car shows with a borrowed point-and-shoot. Still doing it for fun, not as a job.

Most of what you'll see here is cars (meets, rolling shots, late-night detail stuff) and astronomy when I can get away from light pollution. Same camera, same hobby brain.

Everything's on a Nikon D7100. I like knowing the camera well instead of chasing gear.

If I photographed your car at a meet, check Your Photos — I usually drop galleries there so you can download the shots. Otherwise, poke around the galleries and grab whatever you like.
