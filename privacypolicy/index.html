Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications developed by Nathan Fischer, including but not limited to Simply Click, GlobalTag, and Portfolio10.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information unless explicitly stated within the app. Some apps may request permission to access your device’s location (e.g., GlobalTag) to enable core features. This data is stored locally and not shared with us or any third party. Portfolio10 is the one exception: if you choose to sign in and publish your portfolio publicly, some data is sent to and stored on our servers so other users can discover it — see the dedicated Portfolio10 section below for exactly what that includes.

2. Location Data

Some apps, such as GlobalTag, use your device’s location to provide key features like tagging and proximity notifications. Your location is never sent to a server or shared externally. It is only used within the app and stored on your device. Portfolio10 is again the exception — see below.

3. Photos and Media

Apps may allow attaching photos or accessing your gallery (e.g., when tagging locations, or building a photo portfolio). These photos remain on your device and are never uploaded or shared unless explicitly stated. In Portfolio10, photos you choose for your portfolio stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync (stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple’s CloudKit, never on our servers) or you choose to sign in and publish your portfolio publicly, which does send your photos to our servers so other users can view them in the app’s Discover feature — see below.

4. Portfolio10 Online Features (Optional)

Signing in and publishing your portfolio publicly in Portfolio10 are both entirely optional — the app works fully offline without either. If you do sign in (with Sign in with Apple or an email/password account) and choose to publish, the following is sent to and stored on our servers, hosted on Supabase:

• Your account identifier, and your email address if you use email sign-in.
• Your published portfolio: username, display name, bio, chosen niches, city/region/country, profile picture, social media links, and each published photo along with any title, description, and camera/lens/EXIF details you’ve added.
• If you use “Use My Location” while publishing, a one-time coarse location lookup to fill in your city/region/country and enable “Nearby” search — we do not track your location in the background or continuously.
• Follows, likes, and the follower/following/like counts shown on a profile. Direct messages between accounts that follow each other (or with our in-app support account) are stored on our servers and readable by both participants in a conversation.
• If you grant notification permission, a device token so we can notify you about new follows, likes, and messages.
• If you block another account or report a profile/photo, that action — and any details you write in a report — is stored and visible only to us for review, never to other users.

You can permanently delete your published portfolio, or your entire account, at any time from within the app’s Settings. Deleting your account removes everything listed above from our servers.

5. Analytics & Advertising

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools across our apps generally. Portfolio10 is the exception: it shows advertising to free users, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads, but only if you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt — declining still shows ads, just not personalized ones. See Google’s privacy policy for what Google collects. Purchasing Portfolio10 Pro removes all advertising. If any other app begins using analytics or advertising, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

6. In-App Purchases

Some apps offer in-app purchases (e.g., unlocking Premium features, or Portfolio10 Pro). These transactions are securely handled by Apple’s App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

7. Your Data

All user data is stored locally on your device unless otherwise specified. You can delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Portfolio10 offers an optional iCloud Sync feature that stores your portfolio — including your photos — in your own private iCloud account via Apple’s CloudKit, accessible only to you through your Apple ID and never to us, and an optional online account for publishing your portfolio publicly (see the Portfolio10 Online Features section above) — data tied to that account can be deleted from within the app at any time. No other app currently uses cloud storage or requires an account.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or any app, please contact us at nathan@fischdesigns.com, or, for Portfolio10 specifically, via the in-app Contact Support feature in the Messages tab.
